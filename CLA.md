# Contributing rights to Kortyx

Before we merge a contribution, its contributors must agree to the
[Kortyx Contributor License Agreement, version 1.0](.github/cla/1.0.md).

**You keep your copyright.** You grant Mustafa Sait Önal, operating as Kortyx,
rights to use your contribution in Kortyx Cloud and other commercial products,
and to sublicense or relicense it under open-source, source-available, or
proprietary terms. Those rights may transfer to a future Kortyx company or
successor. Read the full agreement before signing.

On your pull request, the **CLA / agreement** check posts a link to the exact
agreement and an acceptance statement containing its SHA-256 hash. Read that
version and post the statement yourself as a new comment. Every human PR author,
commit author, and identified co-author must sign. A signature applies to that
version and future contributions covered by it; you do not need to sign every PR.
The check records your acceptance in the public `cla-signatures` branch. Do not
include private information in a public comment.

If GitHub cannot identify a commit author, associate the commit email with the
author's GitHub account or correct the attribution and push again. Use GitHub's
`ID+username@users.noreply.github.com` address for co-author trailers. Unknown
identities fail the check; a maintainer cannot sign on someone else's behalf.
For employer-owned work, third-party material, or another signing arrangement,
contact **legal@kortyx.io** before proceeding. A separate arrangement needs
maintainer review and implementation before the automated gate can recognize it.

This agreement does not change the repository's current
[licenses](LICENSES.md), take ownership of someone else's work, retroactively
license past contributions, or cancel licenses already granted to users.

Maintainers: see the [CLA operations guide](.github/cla/OPERATIONS.md).
